Methodology — how every number on this board is derived

Nothing here is a hardcoded list of names. The board is computed from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot alone (fetched once; its date is in the header), in four steps a reader can reproduce:

  1. Find the pre-token set. A protocol is a launch candidate when its DefiLlama record carries no token — both its CoinGecko id and its symbol are empty. The board ranks every such protocol holding at least $10M of TVL. The house watchlist (no token, at least $50M of TVL, not a wrapped or bridged product) is marked with a star and is always on the board: it is the floor, never the ceiling.
  2. Price a dollar of TVL by category. For each category, take every protocol whose token already trades and compute market cap ÷ TVL; the category multiple is the median, shrunk toward the global median by peer count so thin categories lean on the whole market. A lending dollar and a bridge dollar are worth different amounts at launch, and the snapshot itself says by how much.
  3. Adjust for the protocol, not the story. Momentum scales the value by the seven-day TVL change, clamped so one noisy week moves it at most −15% to +36%. Maturity scales it by how long the protocol has run with no token, up to +12% at six years: time live means users a launch would have to reward. The class factor then asks what a token could actually claim — a wrapper or staking product of an asset that already trades could only tokenise its fees (×0.03), a bridge conduit's TVL is other protocols' assets in transit (×0.15), and a versioned deployment beside its flagship takes half (×0.5).
  4. Rank and show the working. Rows sort by implied value; every row carries its multiple, its factors and its peer count, and the detail view shows the arithmetic for that protocol. Where the snapshot is silent (no seven-day change, no listing date) the factor is neutral and the detail view says so.

An implied value is this board's own model claim about a token that does not exist. It is not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when, and it is not a price anyone has offered. Protocols the record shows with no token may still have one the record does not carry.
